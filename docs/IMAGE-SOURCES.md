# Image sources and licences

Neither website shows photo credits. Every photograph listed here is under a licence that allows commercial use without visible attribution, and this file is the record of where each one came from.

- **Pexels License** (https://www.pexels.com/license/): free for commercial use, editing allowed, and no credit required. Photos must not suggest that the people shown endorse Boctrust. Identifiable people must not be shown in a way that is offensive or misleading.
- **CC0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): public domain dedication, so no conditions apply.

No licence covers the consent of identifiable people to appear in bank advertising. Legal should confirm that this is acceptable before launch.

The CC BY and CC BY-SA photographs used earlier (WOCinTech Chat, Wikimedia Commons city and market photos) require visible credit. All of them were replaced instead of being kept without credit.
